WHAT ARE THE
COMMONWEALTH GAMES?

The Commonwealth Games are an international multi-sport event involving athletes and teams from across the Commonwealth.

The Games bring together athletes from different nations and territories to compete in sports ranging from athletics and swimming to boxing, badminton, wrestling and weightlifting.

Beyond competition, the Games celebrate friendship, sporting excellence, diversity and the shared connections between Commonwealth communities.
